              How it works

              The 5× workflow

              Your Curtain Lights 2 (H70B3) has 20 × 26 LEDs. You draw on a 100 × 130 canvas, five times bigger. Each 5 × 5 block becomes one LED, and its color is the average of the block. The faint grid shows these blocks.

              This is what makes animations look smooth instead of blocky. A soft edge that half covers a block lights that LED at half strength. Shapes moved by one cell slide a fifth of an LED, so motion glides between beads. Use the soft brush, gradients, glow and tweening, and keep Snap to palette at 0 for the smoothest result.

              The Panel preview shows the exact output, as glowing LEDs or flat pixels. Panel view (Q) shows it on the big canvas too.

              Tips for good-looking animations

              • Black is "off". Dark shapes only show against lit areas, so put silhouettes in front of a moon or a glowing sky.
              • Keep shapes big and simple: 20 LEDs is narrow. Bold outlines read better than fine detail.
              • Use 60–100 ms per frame for smooth motion and Hold for pauses.
              • Put backgrounds on their own layer. Add glow on an Add layer above anything that should shine.
              • Onion skin (U) shows neighbouring frames while you draw.
              • The fastest route to pro-looking results: Import a short video or GIF (a Christmas clip, an animated sticker, or an animation made with an AI video app). Pick 2–4 seconds with one clear subject, use Crop position to frame it, and keep Curtain look on.

              Export and upload

              1. Press Export and check the size and frame count. Over 100 frames or 100 KB shows a warning. Govee does not publish limits; owners estimate the curtain holds about 100 KB, and fewer colors (in Export) shrink a GIF a lot.
              2. On iPhone, tap Share / Save Image → Save Image. On a computer, Download GIF and move it to your phone (AirDrop or iCloud Drive).
              3. In Govee Home, open your Curtain Lights 2 and turn it on. Go to DIY, tap +, choose the Simple tab, set the effect to GIF, and tap the tick.
              4. Tap + under Upload GIF, pick it from Photos (the Animated album), then tap Apply.
              5. If Govee says Application failed, stay close to the curtain (it uploads over Bluetooth), reconnect, and apply again.
              6. Auto-Play in Govee switches effects only every 5 minutes and doesn't resume after a timer turns the lights back on, so start it again by hand.

              Save the Project file too, so you can reopen and edit later.
